JG Explains Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who we are

JG Explains is a small educational video channel. "JG Explains pipeline" is a private tool used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos.

What the app does

The app uses Google sign-in (OAuth) and the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the JG Explains YouTube channel, and
  • read view and engagement statistics for that same channel.

It is not offered to the public. It does not access any other person's account.

What data we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data from viewers or anyone else. The only data the app handles is the channel owner's own YouTube data listed above. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time at Google Account permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Comments and messages

If you comment on a JG Explains video, your comment is handled by YouTube or Instagram under their own policies. We may reply to it or use your question as an idea for a future video, without sharing your personal details.
